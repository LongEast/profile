## Day & Night cat walk cycle

`public/assets/v3/cat-walk.svg` is original monochrome vector artwork created
for this portfolio. Its four-frame walk-cycle rhythm was informed by
[2D cat sprite](https://opengameart.org/content/2d-cat-sprite) by Noob (2017),
which is released under **CC0** / public domain dedication. The local asset is
not a downloaded or embedded copy of the upstream PNG; it is a reworked,
black-and-white SVG sprite atlas made for Day & Night. No attribution is
required by CC0, but this notice preserves the provenance.
